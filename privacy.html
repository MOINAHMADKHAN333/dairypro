1. Offline-First Local Storage

DairyPro stores milk collection entries, FAT/SNF quality records, farmer Khata passbooks, Pashu Ahar stock, and cashbook transactions locally on the operator device. No personal or financial data is sold to advertisers.

2. Role-Based Access Control

DairyPro isolates Centre Admin, Staff Operator, and Farmer Passbook sessions. Producers can access only their own milk pours and settlement balances using their Farmer Code and 4-digit PIN.

3. Hardware & External Actions

Bluetooth and Serial permissions are used strictly to connect thermal receipt printers and milk analyzers/scales. WhatsApp slip sharing and UPI payment links are triggered only when clicked by the user.
