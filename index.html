🛡️ DairyPro Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: November 2026 • Application: DairyPro (Commercial Milk Collection & Dairy OS)

1. Offline-First Local Data Storage

DairyPro operates on an offline-first architecture. All milk collection records, FAT/SNF readings, producer profiles, bank/UPI payout details, Pashu Ahar stock, and cashbook entries are stored locally on the centre's device (localStorage) and are never sold or shared with third-party advertising networks.

2. Role-Based Access & Producer Privacy

Access to DairyPro is strictly separated between Centre Admin, Staff Operators, and Milk Producers (Farmers). Farmers sign in with their individual Farmer Code and 4-digit PIN and can view only their own milk pours, quality tests, and Khata passbook.

3. Hardware & Optional Permissions

Web Bluetooth and Web Serial permissions are requested strictly when the operator pairs a 58mm/80mm thermal receipt printer or digital weighing scale/milk analyzer. WhatsApp/SMS slip sharing and UPI payout links are initiated solely on user action.

4. Data Backup, Deletion & Contact

Centre Admins can export full JSON backups, CSV billing sheets, or permanently wipe local device records at any time from the Centre Setup and Reset Data controls. For privacy inquiries, contact the Dairy Centre Administrator listed on your milk receipt slip.
